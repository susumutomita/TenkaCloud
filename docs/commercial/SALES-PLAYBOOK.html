TenkaCloud — Commercial Sales Playbook

One page per package. Use these in qualifying calls, when writing a follow-up email, or when handing off an inbound to a collaborator.

Companion to: PACKAGES.html (scope source of truth)
Tracks: #1354
Audience: Anyone fielding a TenkaCloud inquiry — including the maintainer, collaborators, community advocates.

How to use this playbook

The packages page (PACKAGES.html) is the formal scope document. This page is the conversational one: how to describe a package in 30 seconds, what to ask a prospect, what to answer when they push back, and what the next step looks like. Read it before a qualifying call; copy the elevator into a follow-up email; share a single section as a link.

Three rules before any call

  1. OSS is free. Don't sell it. If the buyer wants to self-host on their own AWS account, point them at the README and the landing page Quickstart. Make it easy for them to leave the call as a happy OSS user — they will come back when they need facilitation.
  2. Productized vs. consulting. If the conversation drifts toward open-ended advisory work, that is CCoE Enablement — surface it explicitly, do not fold it into Hosted Event or Annual Arena.
  3. No production access, ever. Custom Problem is a sanitized scenario. If the buyer wants a replica of their production system with live data, stop the conversation and route to security review before continuing.

Hosted Event One-page sell sheet

30-second elevator

TenkaCloud is an OSS cloud-drill platform that runs hands-on AWS competitions on each team's isolated AWS environment. With Hosted Event, we run a 1-3 day drill for you, end to end — we pick the problems from the public catalog, deploy into your AWS account, dry-run it, facilitate the live event, and hand you a post-event report. You get one credible event without building the platform yourself, and the OSS is yours forever to keep running internally.

Qualifying questions

How many participants and teams?Up to ~30 fits comfortably; 30-60 is doable; > 60 should be quoted as a custom event.
When is the event?Need ~4 weeks lead time. Less than 2 weeks → decline or scope down.
Do you have an AWS account you can dedicate to this?Default is buyer-provided. If no, quote AWS account provisioning as a line item.
Public catalog OK, or do you need company-specific problems?Public-only → pure Hosted Event. Company-specific → Hosted Event + Custom Problem line item.
One-shot, or first of many?If "first of many," surface Annual Arena early — better economics for both sides.
Internal change-management work expected?If yes → flag CCoE Enablement as a separate conversation; do not fold in.

Common objections

"Can we just self-host?"Yes — that is the OSS path and we encourage it. Hosted Event is for organizations that want the operational guarantee of a facilitated event without the platform learning curve.
"It's too expensive vs. a free CTF."Hosted Event delivers an event on your AWS account with real isolated infrastructure per team, branded portal, scoring, and a written post-event report. CTF SaaS does not provision real AWS for participants.
"Can you fly out / be on-site?"Yes — quoted as a separate travel + day-rate line item, not bundled into the base fee.
"What if the platform breaks mid-event?"Dry run catches almost everything. Live event has a real-time on-call commitment (= 30-min hot-fix target, see SLA-lite in PACKAGES.html). Worst case, fall back to the public OSS catalog manually.
"Why isn't pricing on your website?"Public starting price is on the landing page. Final quote depends on size, mix, and AWS-account ownership.
Next step: "Want to lock the date? I'll send a one-page SoW with the problem mix and the dry-run window. Tell me the rough participant count and your preferred date window." Link the prospect to PACKAGES.html — Hosted Event for the formal scope.

Annual Arena One-page sell sheet

30-second elevator

Annual Arena turns TenkaCloud into a year-long capability program. We run 4-6 operated events for your organization, grow a private problem catalog that reflects your stack, and give your HR / L&D team a KPI dashboard they can show to leadership. It is the right fit when "we want one event" turns into "we want a credible CCoE training program on an annual budget line."

Qualifying questions

Do you have an annual L&D / CCoE budget line, or is this discretionary spend?Annual Arena belongs on an annual line. Discretionary → start with Hosted Event.
How many cohorts / audiences?3-4 cohorts (new grad, mid, platform team) is typical. More is fine; fewer probably means Hosted Event × 2-3 is cheaper.
Do you have a private catalog wishlist?If yes → surface Custom Problem line items inside the contract. If no → defer until Q2.
Who reads the KPI dashboard?If "the CCoE lead" → standard dashboard. If "the CTO / VP of Engineering" → we structure the metrics differently.
Renewal expectation?3-year horizon is healthiest. 1-year-only buyers may be better served by Hosted Event × N.
Internal change-management expected?If yes → offer CCoE Enablement as a parallel retainer, not folded in.

Common objections

"4-6 events feels like a lot."Most buyers settle on 4 in year one. Six is the upper end for very large orgs (new-grad cohort + mid cohort + platform deep-dive + executive demo).
"Why pay annually instead of buying Hosted Event each time?"Annual Arena adds the program-level deliverables (private catalog, KPI dashboard, quarterly review, two waived Custom Problem design fees) that don't exist in standalone Hosted Event. Per-event price is also lower.
"Can we own the private catalog?"Yes — the buyer owns IP of the private catalog. We curate it; we do not own it.
"What if we miss an event date?"Event slots are reschedulable inside the contract year with 4 weeks notice. Slots not used by year-end do not roll over (otherwise the contract becomes unbounded).
"Is this a SaaS subscription?"No. The platform is OSS and runs on your AWS. You are paying for operated events + program-level deliverables, not for software access.
Next step: "Let's do a 30-min program-design session: cohorts, cadence, and what the KPI dashboard needs to show. I'll come back with a draft program memo before we talk pricing." Link to PACKAGES.html — Annual Arena.

Custom Problem One-page sell sheet

30-second elevator

A Custom Problem is a single hands-on drill authored to match your stack or a past incident. We do a 60-minute scenario interview, design the problem with you, implement it as a self-contained directory (metadata.json + template.yaml), validate it end-to-end against a real AWS account, and hand it over. You own the IP. It runs on the same OSS platform any TenkaCloud event uses.

Qualifying questions

What is the drill about in one sentence?If they can't say it in a sentence, the scope is not ready — do a discovery call first.
Past incident, or "we want our teams to know X"?Past incident → richer scenario, faster build. Capability gap → longer design phase.
Which AWS services need to be in the problem?Map to the 5-kind decision tree (flag / uptime-flat / uptime-multi / phased-polling / attack-detection).
Will you run the problem yourself, or do you also want us to facilitate?Just the asset → Custom Problem only. Also operate it → Custom Problem + Hosted Event.
Public catalog contribution OK?If yes → lower price (we get to ship to the OSS catalog). If no → private delivery, standard price.
Does the design require production access?If yes → stop and route to security review. Custom Problem is sanitized scenarios only.

Common objections

"Can we mirror our production architecture exactly?"We can mirror the shape (services, interactions, failure modes). We never mirror data, secrets, or live credentials. Sanitized scenarios only.
"How realistic can the scenario be?"Very — most engagements deliver a problem indistinguishable from a real on-call exercise. Realism is in the failure modes, not in the data.
"What if the scenario grows during build?"The scope brief from the scenario interview is the source of truth. Growth beyond it is a revision line item, not a free addition.
"Can you maintain the problem as our stack evolves?"Yes, as a revision (typically 25-50% of the original fee per revision cycle). Maintenance is not bundled.
"Why not just write it ourselves?"You can — the OSS toolchain is documented and there is an AGENT.md / SKILL.md for AI agents to scaffold problems. Custom Problem is for buyers who want the design + scoring + dry-run done by someone who has shipped many of these.
Next step: "Let's book the 60-minute scenario interview. Come with 1-3 candidate scenarios and rough success criteria for each — I'll come back with a scope brief and which of the 5 scoring kinds fits." Link to PACKAGES.html — Custom Problem.

CCoE Enablement One-page sell sheet

30-second elevator

CCoE Enablement is a monthly advisory retainer for buyers who need help with the operating model around their cloud enablement — not just events. Weekly working sessions, a monthly written deliverable, async Q&A in between. Sold separately from event delivery so events stay productized. If you want both events and advisory, that is two line items, not one.

Qualifying questions

What does success look like in 6 months?If "we ran events" → not CCoE Enablement; that is Hosted Event or Annual Arena. If "the org operates differently" → CCoE Enablement.
Who is the executive sponsor inside your org?No sponsor = no political traction = retainer will not land. Decline politely.
Have you tried internal change before?If "yes and it failed" → what was missing? Usually it's a written operating model and an external second voice.
Are you also running TenkaCloud events?If yes → great, the two pair well as separate contracts. If no → flag that we are not a generic strategy consultancy; we are TenkaCloud's authors offering advisory adjacent to the platform we ship.
Expected duration?Default 3-6 months. < 3 months rarely produces enough artifacts to land internally.

Common objections

"Why isn't this bundled with Annual Arena?"Bundling turns Annual Arena into open-ended consulting. The clarity of the productized service is its value — and a small team cannot deliver predictable events and predictable consulting under one bill.
"You're not a Big-4 strategy firm."Correct — and that is the point. The advisory is grounded in shipping a working OSS cloud-training platform, not slide decks. Buyers who want a Big-4 deliverable should use Big 4.
"Can we just hire you for a one-off workshop?"No. A one-day workshop without a follow-through retainer does not produce anything that lands internally. Either run it as a Hosted Event (and call it that), or commit to the minimum 3-month retainer.
"Will you write our internal political comms?"No. We supply the artifacts (operating model, roadmap, KPI definitions); landing them with stakeholders is the buyer's responsibility.
Next step: "Let's do a 30-min sponsor alignment call — I want to make sure there's an executive sponsor on your side before we sign anything. If there is, I'll send a 3-month retainer SoW." Link to PACKAGES.html — CCoE Enablement.
